# Colors

Use role tokens, never the raw ramps. Each role has a Tailwind class: `bg-surface-*`, `text-text-*`, `border-border-*`, `bg-status-*` and `text-status-*`, `*-chart-*`.

## Surface

Backgrounds for the page, cards, overlays and brand areas.

<ColorGroup prefix="surface-" />

## Text

The ratio is against white. Body text needs AA (4.5:1). `text-brand` is decorative (Eyebrow accent only), so the heading next to it must carry the meaning. `text-on-brand` is for brand and inverse surfaces, so its ratio on white does not apply. `text-faint` and `text-disabled` are for placeholder and disabled states only.
