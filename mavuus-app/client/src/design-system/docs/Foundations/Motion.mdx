# Motion

Motion explains a change; it never decorates. Use the duration and easing tokens instead of literal values: `ease-enter` for things arriving, `ease-exit` for things leaving, `ease-move` for things moving on screen.

<MotionTokens />

## Reduced motion

- Every animation or transition that moves things gets `motion-reduce:` handling.
- Scroll reveals use `patterns/Reveal`. With reduced motion, the `[data-reveal]` rules in `styles/motion.css` fall back to a short opacity fade with no transform, clip or filter.
- JS that depends on the preference reads `useReducedMotion()` from `hooks/`. Charts and CountUp turn their animation off with it.
- Stagger is capped: 70ms per step, 420ms at most.
